1. Overview

LudoCraft ("we", "us", "our", or the "App") is a multiplayer board game published by LogicWeave Studios ("Developer"). This Privacy Policy applies to the App available on Google Play and describes our practices regarding the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of information when you install, access, or play the game.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Data we collect

We collect only what's needed to run matches, keep accounts secure, and improve the game.

Information you provide

  • Account details — display name, avatar, and profile photo if you sign in with Google Play Games, Facebook, or a similar provider.
  • Contact information — email address, only if you contact support or opt into newsletters.
  • User content — chat messages sent to other players during a match, and any support tickets or feedback you submit.

Information collected automatically

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), language, and time zone.
  • Gameplay data — match history, dice rolls, win/loss records, coins and in-game currency balances, level progress, and session duration.
  • Log and diagnostic data — crash reports, app performance metrics, IP address, and approximate location derived from IP (city/region level only).
  • Usage analytics — screens viewed, buttons tapped, and feature engagement, collected in aggregate to improve the game.

Information from third parties

  • Basic profile data from sign-in providers (Google, Facebook) if you choose social login.
  • Ad performance data from our advertising and attribution partners, listed in Section 6.

3. Device permissions

The App may request the following Android permissions. You can review or revoke these at any time in your device settings.

  • Internet access — required to play online multiplayer matches.
  • Network state — used to detect connectivity and reconnect you to matches.
  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback on dice rolls and moves.
  • Storage (optional) — used only if you choose to save or share a screenshot of your board.
  • Notifications — used to alert you about your turn, friend requests, and daily rewards, if enabled.

4. How we use your data

  • To operate core gameplay — matchmaking, turn sequencing, and syncing game state in real time.
  • To maintain your profile, progress, coins, and leaderboard standing.
  • To detect and prevent cheating, bots, and abusive behavior between players.
  • To respond to support requests and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To personalize content, such as suggested opponents or relevant offers.
  • To measure app performance and fix crashes through diagnostic logs.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

5. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data only with:

  • Service providers — cloud hosting (e.g. Google Firebase / AWS), analytics, and crash-reporting vendors bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Other players — your display name, avatar, and level are visible to opponents during a match; chat messages are visible to players in that match.
  • Advertising partners — anonymized/pseudonymous identifiers for ad delivery and measurement, if the App shows ads (see Section 6).
  • Legal authorities — where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers — if the App or studio is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

6. Ads & analytics

The App may display banner, interstitial, or rewarded video ads through third-party ad networks, and may use analytics SDKs to understand app usage. These partners may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Meta Audience Network
  • Unity Ads
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics

These providers may collect device identifiers (like the Advertising ID) and usage data to serve relevant ads and measure performance, under their own privacy policies. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads menu, or by resetting your Advertising ID.

7. Purchases & billing

All in-app purchases (coins, gems, ad-removal, cosmetic items, etc.) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card number, bank details, or full billing address. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed and its associated order ID, which we use to credit your in-game account.

8. Data retention

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward in case you return. Diagnostic logs are typically retained for 90 days. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see Section 11.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us using the details in Section 14.

11. Your rights & choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including personalized ads.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at studios.logicweave@gmail.com or use the in-app Settings → Delete Account option. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

12. International data transfers

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States / Singapore / European Union. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect data transferred internationally.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
